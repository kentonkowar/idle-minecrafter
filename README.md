# Idle Minecrafter

Idle Minecrafter is a Minecraft Java Edition mod that grants offline progress to your player-built farms, quarries, and crafting setups. Log off and shut your computer down, then return to the resources you need for the next day.

## Why Idle Minecrafter?

Minecraft requires you to stay logged in for your farms, quarries, and crafting setups to produce. That means your computer runs all night, stays loud and power-hungry, and your idle character can die. Idle games solved this decades ago by calculating offline progress when you return. Now Minecraft can too!

## Features (v1.0)

- **Offline production**: Register your setups and earn output during your absence based on how they actually perform while you're playing
- **Three setup types**: Mob farms, quarries (block mining), and crafting stations all work offline
- **Fair rates**: Offline rates come from real observations of your setups.
- **Smart limits**: Offline crafting respects input scarcity and storage limits
- **Catch-up summary**: See what was produced and where it went when you return
- **Configurable**: Single-player worlds can set offline-time caps and toggle features on or off
- **Safe**: The mod respects Minecraft saves and works perfectly after uninstalling

## How Registration Works

Each setup begins with an **Idle Controller**—a special block that anchors your registration. You use it to:

1. **Select chunks** — Mark the region where your setup is built (using the Surveyor tool or the controller's map)
2. **Link storage** — Point the controller to where output goes and where inputs come from (using Idle Storage blocks or linked vanilla containers like chests)
3. **Confirm** — Finalize the setup. Nothing is tracked until you confirm

The Surveyor tool lets you mark chunks directly in the world while building. For larger regions (like quarries), the controller's built-in map shows a top-down view of your chunks.

**Storage** comes in 9 tiers, from Wood to the new Collapsed Nether Star. Higher tiers hold more offline output, so you can leave your setup running longer without hitting capacity limits.

## How Offline Output Works

1. **Register a setup** while playing with the Idle Controller and Surveyor
2. **Play normally**: The mod observes what your setup actually produces, measuring fair rates
3. **Log off and enjoy**: No computer running, no noise, no power cost
4. **Return anytime**: On login, offline progress is calculated instantly and delivered to your registered storage
